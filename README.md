# 📊 Global Superstore Sales & Business Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Global Superstore Sales & Business Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing global retail transaction data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and regional business trends.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, starting from data preparation and exploratory analysis to SQL-based business analysis and interactive Power BI reporting.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📈 Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- 💰 Evaluate profitability across different business areas
- 🛍️ Identify high-performing and low-performing products
- 📦 Analyze category and sub-category performance
- 🌍 Compare market, region, and country-level performance
- 👥 Analyze customer segment performance
- 🏆 Identify top customers and products
- 📅 Analyze yearly and monthly sales trends
- 🚚 Analyze shipping modes and order priority
- 🔎 Understand the relationship between discount and profitability
- 📊 Present business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard

## 📂 Dataset

The Global Superstore dataset contains transactional retail data covering customers, products, categories, markets, regions, countries, sales, profit, shipping, and order information.

### 📋 Dataset Information

The dataset includes information related to:

- 🆔 Order and customer details
- 📅 Order and shipping dates
- 🚚 Shipping modes
- 👥 Customer segments
- 🌍 Countries, markets, and regions
- 🛍️ Products and categories
- 📦 Sub-categories
- 💵 Sales and profit
- 🔢 Quantity
- 🏷️ Discount
- 🚢 Shipping cost
- ⚡ Order priority
